Privacy Policy

ZERO-MIND collects only information reasonably needed to operate its public research, forms, digital offers and payment-related support.

Information we may process

  • Information you voluntarily submit through a ZERO-MIND form, such as an email address and message.
  • Basic traffic and conversion measurements used to understand whether public pages are useful.
  • Payment status or transaction references provided by the payment processor when a paid offer is used.

Service providers

Public traffic measurement may use GoatCounter. Form delivery may use Web3Forms. Payments may be processed through Razorpay. Payment card, UPI or bank credentials are entered into the payment provider's interface and are not intended to be stored by ZERO-MIND.

Use and retention

Information is used for fulfilment, customer support, security, measurement, accounting and legal compliance. ZERO-MIND does not sell personal information. Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for these purposes or as required by law.
