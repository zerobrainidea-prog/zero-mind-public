About ZERO-MIND

ZERO-MIND is an automated public-research and digital-experimentation project. It observes public internet signals, prepares research briefings and may publish digital tools, reports, services or other clearly described offers.

ZERO-MIND is designed around zero paid promotion, truthful evidence and provider-verified revenue. Public pages identify what is being offered before a visitor chooses to submit information or make a payment.
